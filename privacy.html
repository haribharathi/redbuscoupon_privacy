redBus Coupon Finder

Privacy Policy — Last updated: October 2026

redBus Coupon Finder is a Chrome extension that helps you find working coupon codes on redBus payment pages. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, why, and what happens to it.

What data is accessed

  • redBus order information (browser cookie): The extension reads a redBus session cookie (COResp / updatedCOResp) that contains your current booking details (route, journey date, seat). This data is used locally in your browser to test whether a coupon applies to your booking. It is never sent to our servers.
  • Coupon codes you submit: If you use the "Suggest a coupon" feature, the coupon code and whether it worked is sent to our backend to be added to the shared coupon list. No personal information (name, email, account details) is included.

What data is stored on our servers

Only anonymous coupon codes and their aggregate usage counts are stored. No personal data, no account information, and no browsing history is ever stored or transmitted to our servers.

Third-party services

To test whether a coupon is valid, the extension calls redBus's own order API (redbus.in) using your existing session. This is the same request redBus makes when you apply a coupon manually. No data is shared with any other third party.

Permissions used and why

  • activeTab: Required to read the current payment page and detect when you are on a redBus booking page.
  • host_permissions for redbus.in: Required to run the coupon-testing content script on redBus pages.
  • host_permissions for our backend: Required for the background service worker to fetch the coupon list and submit suggested codes without CORS restrictions.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties.
